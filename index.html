📋 Emergency Phrases (Sicilian/Italian)
English → Italian:
• "Help!" → "Aiuto!"
• "Call ambulance" → "Chiami un'ambulanza"
• "I'm injured" → "Sono ferito/a"
• "Heart attack" → "Infarto"
• "Accident" → "Incidente"
💡 Tips for Emergencies
✓ Always dial 112 first for life-threatening situations
✓ Stay calm and speak slowly
